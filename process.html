How It Works

Six straightforward, transparent steps from your first inquiry to your first day on the job abroad.

Step-By-Step Journey

A Structured, Predictable Path

We eliminate guesswork. Here is the exact path every successful applicant follows with The Moffats.

1

Initial Consultation & Profile Screening

Reach out via phone, WhatsApp, or visit our Nairobi office. We review your work history, skills, language abilities, and understand your country preference (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Iraq, or Lebanon, as well as visit visas for Canada, Schengen Europe, and Turkiye).

2

Document Assembly & Verification

We confirm all mandatory paperwork is in order: valid Kenyan passport, DCI Certificate of Good Conduct, clean white-background photos, full-length professional photo, and brief video introduction where required.

3

Accredited GAMCA Medical Screening

You undergo comprehensive medical testing at a certified GCC/GAMCA medical center in Nairobi (routine blood work, chest X-ray, general physical) to obtain your Fit-to-Work clearance certificate.

4

Employer Matching & Official Contract

Your profile is submitted to vetted employers. Upon selection, an official employment offer is issued clearly detailing your salary, working hours, accommodation, food, and leave entitlements for your review.

5

Work Visa Approval & Flight Booking

We process your legal work visa with the host country’s Ministry of Interior. Once the visa is issued and verified, we book your direct flight ticket and conduct a thorough pre-departure briefing.

6

Airport Welcome & Workplace Onboarding

You board your flight from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA). Upon arrival in Doha, Dubai, Muscat, Riyadh, or your destination city, our licensed ground liaison meets you at the arrivals gate and safely transfers you to your residence.

Preparation Checklist

What You Need to Apply

Ensure you have these primary documents ready to fast-track your overseas placement.

🛂

Valid Kenyan Passport

Must have at least 6 months validity from the intended date of departure and at least two blank pages.

🛡️

Good Conduct Certificate

Valid Certificate of Good Conduct issued by the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI / eCitizen).

🩺

GAMCA Medical Report

Official "Fit to Work" medical report from an approved Gulf GCC Medical Center in Nairobi.

📸

Passport & Full Photos

Recent color passport-size photos (white background) and one neat, decent full-length photograph.

📜

Certificates & CV

Any relevant vocational certificates (caregiver certificate, driving license, plumbing, electrical, housekeeping).

🎥

Brief Introduction Video

A short 1-minute video introducing yourself, your experience, and readiness (our team helps you record this!).

⚠️
