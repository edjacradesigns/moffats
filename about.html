About The Moffats

A trusted, Nairobi-rooted bridge connecting hardworking Kenyan ambition with verified, dignified opportunities in the Gulf.

Who We Are

Empowering Kenyans through legitimate overseas pathways.

The Moffats was founded in Nairobi with one uncompromising goal: to bring honesty, security, and professional excellence to the overseas employment journey.

Too many Kenyans seeking opportunities abroad face predatory middlemen, hidden fees, and misleading contract terms. We built The Moffats to offer a dignified alternative. We do not just hand you a visa — we walk beside you through passport acquisition, GAMCA medical certifications, embassy attestations, flight arrangements, and on-ground settlement in Qatar and Oman.

Our team in Nairobi maintains direct contact with verified employers, licensed labor recruitment partners, and community coordinators across Doha, Muscat, and beyond.
