Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019

The Moffats ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, handle, process, and protect personal information submitted through this website and during your overseas recruitment process.

1. Information We Collect

To evaluate your candidacy and process your overseas employment, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Identification: Full name, national identity number, passport details, date of birth, and nationality.
  • Contact Information: Phone number, WhatsApp contact, residential address, and email address.
  • Professional Details: Curriculum vitae (CV), educational certificates, vocational credentials, past employment history, and references.
  • Verification Documents: Police Clearance (Certificate of Good Conduct from the DCI), medical fitness certificates (GAMCA/GCC approved), and introductory videos/photographs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data strictly for legitimate recruitment and travel facilitation purposes:

  • To evaluate your profile against job specifications from vetted employers in Qatar, Oman, and the Gulf.
  • To coordinate mandatory pre-departure steps, including GAMCA medical appointments and flight bookings.
  • To submit visa and work permit applications to the respective host country ministries of labor and immigration authorities.
  • To communicate progress updates, contract details, and travel itineraries directly to you.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

We only share your information with authorized entities directly involved in your placement process:

  • Vetted, contracted prospective employers and host-country licensed recruitment partners.
  • Immigration authorities, embassies, and consulates for visa issuance and flight ticketing.
  • Accredited medical testing centers and travel service providers.

We will never sell, lease, or monetize your personal data to advertisers or unauthorized commercial third parties.

4. Form Processing & Security

Our website contact and application forms are securely transmitted via FormSubmit to our official recruitment inbox (edjacradesigns@gmail.com). We apply reasonable physical, digital, and administrative safeguards to protect your records against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Your Rights Under Kenya Law

In accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you hold the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold your personal data and access copies of it.
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Request the deletion of your data when it is no longer required for regulatory compliance or active placement.
  • Withdraw your consent to processing at any time, subject to statutory retention obligations.
