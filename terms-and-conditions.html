1. Scope of Recruitment Services

The Moffats acts as an authorized liaison between prospective workers in Kenya and registered employers or licensed placement agencies across Qatar, Oman, and the Gulf. We facilitate document preparation, medical testing appointments, embassy attestations, visa tracking, and flight coordination.

Job specifications, offered wages, allowances, and contractual conditions are set directly by the sponsoring employer in compliance with destination country labor statutes.

2. Candidate Obligations & Declarations

All candidates registering with The Moffats declare and warrant that:

  • All personal information, identification documents, educational certificates, and past employment history provided are genuine, authentic, and complete.
  • They possess a valid Kenyan passport (or are actively applying for one) and are physically and psychologically fit for travel and work abroad.
  • They hold a clean criminal record verified by an official Certificate of Good Conduct from the DCI.
  • They will respect and abide by the labor laws, cultural norms, and legal requirements of both the Republic of Kenya and the host nation.

3. Fee Transparency & Receipts
